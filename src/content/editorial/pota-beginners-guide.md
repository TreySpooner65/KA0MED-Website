Parks on the Air combines portable operation, experimentation, and award chasing in an approachable program for amateur radio operators.

This guide introduces choosing an eligible park, assembling a portable station, spotting an activation, making contacts, and submitting a valid log. It is ready to be expanded with a first-activation checklist and examples from the field.
