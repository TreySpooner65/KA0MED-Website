The end-fed half-wave has become one of the most popular HF antennas for portable and home stations. Its single-ended feed point makes deployment convenient, while a correctly designed matching network lets a transceiver work with the antenna's high feed-point impedance.

This reference article is ready to be expanded with matching-network design, counterpoise considerations, installation patterns, and measured field performance.
