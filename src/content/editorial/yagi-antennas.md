## A flashlight for radio waves

An omnidirectional antenna behaves more like a lantern. A Yagi behaves more like a flashlight, concentrating more of its transmitted and received energy in one direction.

## The basic elements

A typical Yagi uses a driven element, a reflector behind it, and one or more directors in front of it. Their spacing and lengths shape the antenna's directional pattern.
