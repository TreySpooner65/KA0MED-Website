## How a repeater works

A repeater listens on its input frequency and simultaneously retransmits received audio on its output frequency. This allows handheld and mobile radios to communicate across a much larger area than they could directly.

Most repeaters also require a CTCSS tone or DCS code before they will retransmit a signal.
